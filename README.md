# CareerGraph AI

**LangGraph-powered Resume Evaluation, Job Fit Scoring, Cover Letter Generation, and AI Career Coaching Agent.**

CareerGraph AI is an advanced AI career assistant designed to analyze resumes, evaluate their strength against job descriptions, generate professional cover letters, and provide personalized career coaching.  
The system uses **LangGraph** to orchestrate multiple specialized AI agents that reason over resume quality, ATS compatibility, job fit, skill gaps, experience relevance, and career improvement strategy.

This is not a simple resume parser.  
It is a multi-agent career intelligence workflow built for real-world job preparation.
